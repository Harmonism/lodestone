# Harmonism — Doctrinal Backbone (Light)

## The Three Registers
Harmonism operates at three scales of one system, never reducible to any single register. Doctrine (what reality is): metaphysics, epistemology, ontology. The Individual Path (how to live): navigated through the Wheel of Harmony and the Way of Harmony. The Civilizational Scale (how to organize): the Architecture of Harmony, civilizational diagnosis, and dialogue with Western intellectual traditions. The ontological cascade: Logos (the inherent order of the Cosmos) → Dharma (human alignment with Logos) → Harmonism (the philosophical system articulating this alignment) → Applied Harmonism → Harmonics (the lived practice itself).

## Metaphysics
The Absolute (0 + 1 = ∞): the Void (0), the impersonal, absolute aspect of God, pure Being, Transcendence; the Cosmos (1), the divine expression of the Creator, the living, intelligent, patterned Energy Field. Qualified Non-Dualism: reality is ultimately One but expresses through genuine multiplicity.
Harmonic Realism: reality is inherently harmonic, pervaded by Logos, the governing organizing principle of creation. Reality follows a binary pattern at every scale: Void + Cosmos at the Absolute, matter + energy (the 5th Element) within the Cosmos, physical body + energy body in the human being. Never present "physical, vital, mental, spiritual" as the four primary dimensions of the human being; that framing is retired.
Logos has two inseparable registers: structural (the harmonic ordering pattern that recurs as fractal at every scale) and substantive (consciousness met from within, intrinsically self-luminous). One reality, two faces: empirical (natural law, what science studies) and metaphysical (the subtle causal dimension contemplative knowing reaches).
Dharma: human alignment with Logos, held in four dimensions at once: cosmic order, individual purpose (svadharma), ethical rightness in a given situation, natural law. Logos is the order; Dharma is the alignment with that order. Karma names the moral-causal subtle face of Multidimensional Causality, the architecture of consequence. Only Dharma, Logos and karma are adopted as native vocabulary; every other tradition term leads in English with the original appositional. Ṛta and Sangha are retired from native vocabulary.

## The Human Being
The energy body is structured by eight chakras: five Earth (1st–5th), three Sky (6th–8th). Ātman, the 8th, is the soul proper, a fractal of the Absolute. Three centers of consciousness: Peace (Ajna, clear knowing), Love (Anahata, felt connection), Will (Manipura / lower dantian, directed force aligned with Dharma). The destination: Peace, Love and Will collapsing back into undifferentiated Presence.
Five modes of knowing: objective empiricism, subjective empiricism, rational-philosophical, subtle-perceptual, knowledge by identity. No mode adjudicates alone. Discernment integrates across them; the somatic-comfort test ("trust your feelings," "your truth") collapses discernment into the ego-comfort it should disable.
The Five Cartographies of the Soul (Indian, Chinese, Shamanic, Greek, Abrahamic) are held as peer primary. They are convergent witnesses, not sources from which Harmonism is derived.

## The Wheel of Harmony
Eight pillars: Presence the central pillar, seven peripheral pillars around it. The centre is always named the central pillar and always counted: never a bare "seven pillars," never "the other six." Each pillar is itself a sub-wheel of eight spokes (central spoke, then seven peripheral):
Presence (Meditation): Breath/Pranayama, Sound & Silence, Energy/Life Force, Intention, Reflection, Virtue, Entheogens.
Health (Monitor): Sleep, Recovery, Supplementation, Hydration, Purification, Nutrition, Movement.
Matter (Stewardship): Home & Habitat, Transportation & Mobility, Clothing & Personal Items, Technology & Tools, Finance & Wealth, Provisioning & Supply, Security & Protection.
Service (Offering): Vocation, Value Creation, Leadership, Collaboration, Ethics & Accountability, Systems & Operations, Communication & Influence.
Relationships (Love): Couple, Parenting, Family Elders, Friendship, Community, Service to Vulnerable, Communication.
Learning (Wisdom): Philosophy & Sacred Knowledge, Practical Skills, Healing Arts, Gender & Initiation, Communication & Language, Digital Arts, Science & Systems.
Nature (Reverence): Permaculture/Gardens/Trees, Nature Immersion, Water, Earth & Soil, Air & Sky, Animals & Shelter, Ecology & Resilience.
Recreation (Joy): Music, Visual & Plastic Arts, Narrative Arts, Sports & Physical Play, Digital Entertainment, Travel & Adventure, Social Gatherings.
The Way of Harmony is a spiral: Presence → Health → Matter → Service → Relationships → Learning → Nature → Recreation → Presence. A flicker of Presence ignites, Health grounds, Presence deepens as the cleared vessel sustains practice. Every Way follows one pattern: clear before cultivate. Cultivation, not formation.
Priority: Tier 1 Health + Presence; Tier 2 Matter + Service; Tier 3 Relationships (the Crucible); Tier 4 Learning, Nature, Recreation. A collapsed pillar is rebuilt alone before it is combined. Mental-emotional health belongs in Presence; biochemistry in Health. Breath is a Presence pillar. Sleep is the highest-ROI practice in the system. Sleep and vigil timing is proportional and solar, never numerical and civil; the wake time is the lever and the bedtime the value.
