Lodestone

The Harmonist companion, beyond harmonism.io.
All readings: every page Lodestone has read with you, with its address.

Presence

Lodestone comes only when called. Click the Lodestone icon in the toolbar to bring it to the page you are reading, or select a passage and right-click Ask Lodestone about…. Click the icon again, or the × on the sprite, to dismiss it. Until you call it, the extension does not run on the page.

Memory

Signed in to your harmonism.io account, Lodestone remembers what you read and discuss with it here, in the same memory as Lodestone on harmonism.io, on your phone and on your computer. That memory is yours: kept in this browser, carried between your devices by Lodestone sync, encrypted under a key your devices keep and hand to one another, never readable on harmonism.io. Signed out, Lodestone is anonymous here and remembers nothing.
No account? Create one at harmonism.io, confirm it by email, then sign in here.
This browser holds a Lodestone memory from before you signed in here: conversations, and what Lodestone has learned. Is it yours? If it is, it joins your memory. If not, it stays apart, and Lodestone will not ask again.

Your own OpenRouter key

With a key of your own from OpenRouter, Lodestone answers here under it, with no limit on messages and nothing counted against the free ones. harmonism.io prepares each answer as always and hands it back; your browser sends it to OpenRouter, which reads it under your account. Your key never reaches harmonism.io. The key comes from your other devices when they sync it, or you can paste it here.
