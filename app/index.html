Where should Lodestone think?

To think with your computer's model, open Lodestone from that computer: in Lodestone Desktop choose Use from Your Phone… and scan the code with this phone, on the same Wi-Fi. That copy of Lodestone keeps its own conversations; Settings → Export memory and Import memory move them across.

On Android, the Lodestone app pairs with the computer instead: it thinks with the computer's model and can share one memory with it, with nothing leaving your network.

Make a key at openrouter.ai. It goes to OpenRouter and nowhere else, and stays on this device, encrypted; if you choose to sync it, it travels encrypted under your sync passphrase. Harmonia never sees it.

        Using Lodestone

        What this is

        Lodestone is Harmonia's companion. The Harmonism articles and everything you tell it are stored on your own device, and its safeguards run there. The model that answers runs where you choose: on this device or a computer you own, and then nothing you write is sent to anyone; or, if you choose, on harmonism.io's hosted models under your account, or on OpenRouter under your own key, and then each message goes there and nothing else does.

        First use

        Connect to Wi-Fi, choose where Lodestone should think, and press Download and begin. On a computer, This device needs nothing installed and downloads about six gigabytes, once. Keep the page open until it finishes.

        On a phone or tablet

        A phone does not run the model. The models small enough to fit answer badly enough to misrepresent the system they speak for, so that option is not offered — a poor answer in Lodestone's voice is worse than no answer. A phone holds the whole corpus for reading and searching offline, and thinks in one of four ways. Your computer: a model on a computer you own, over your own Wi-Fi; nothing leaves your network. Your harmonism.io account: the models hosted Lodestone runs on, ten messages a day in total across the website, Telegram and Lodestone's apps. OpenRouter: a model you rent with your own key, with no daily limit. No model: a question brings back the passages of the corpus that bear on it, with no network at all. Settings → Change where Lodestone thinks moves between them; your conversations stay.

        Keep it on your phone

        iPhone or iPad: in Safari, tap the Share button, then Add to Home Screen. Safari on iOS 26 or later is required.

        Android: install the Lodestone app (Android 7 or later; allow installing from your browser when Android asks). It is this same app, and it can also pair with Lodestone Desktop on your computer: it then thinks with the computer's model, and Settings → Sync can keep its memory in step with the computer without anything leaving your Wi-Fi. Or, in Chrome, open the menu (three dots) and tap Install app or Add to Home screen.

        Mac or PC: in Chrome or Edge, click the install icon at the right end of the address bar.

        Opened from the home screen, Lodestone works with airplane mode on.

        If the model will not start

        There is no smaller model to fall back to, and that is deliberate: below this size the answers stop being trustworthy in Lodestone's voice, and a confident wrong answer about the system is worse than none. If this computer cannot hold it, or the browser says it has no WebGPU, run the model outside the browser instead — it can hold far more there, and it is the better version anyway. See below.

        Keeping the articles current

        The articles never update by themselves. When you are online, open Settings and press Check for corpus update. Nothing downloads unless you confirm.

        Moving between phone and computer

        Settings → Sync keeps Lodestone on your phone and in Lodestone Desktop on one memory through your harmonism.io account. It is off until you turn it on. Your conversations, notes, Wheel, practices and Harmonic Profile are then kept on harmonism.io, encrypted on each device with a sync passphrase that never leaves it, so harmonism.io stores only what it cannot read. Turn it on once per device with the same account and passphrase; if you forget the passphrase, the synced copy is lost and each device keeps its own.

        Without sync, Settings → Export memory saves one file with your conversations, practices and notes; import it on the other device. Erasing everything is also there and cannot be undone.

        Lodestone Desktop: Lodestone as an app on your computer

        This is the fuller version of Lodestone, and the one a phone uses when it thinks on your own computer. Install Lodestone Desktop like any other application: for Mac with Apple silicon, Mac with Intel, Windows or Linux (about 400 MB, with the whole English library, the retrieval model and the engine included). On first opening it downloads its model once — from 7 GB, chosen for your computer's memory; Lodestone needs at least 16 GB of memory — with a progress bar, and checks it. There is nothing else to install. From then on Lodestone sits in your Applications folder or Start menu, opens in a few seconds, needs no connection, and carries the whole harmonism.io library in English, with other languages a click away.

        The app is not yet signed by Apple or Microsoft, so the first opening asks for confirmation. On a Mac: drag Lodestone from the disk image into Applications, open it once, then System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway. Later versions arrive through Settings → Check for updates. On Windows: More info → Run anyway.

        Using Lodestone from your phone

        To use that computer's model from your phone, choose Use from your phone… in the app's Lodestone menu, switch it on, and scan the code with the phone on the same Wi-Fi. Two consequences worth knowing. The phone then works only while the computer is awake and Lodestone is open on it, and that page cannot be saved for offline use — a plain address on your network is not one browsers will let an app store itself from. And what you tell Lodestone there is kept separately from what you tell the computer, because they are different addresses; Settings → Export and Import move it across.

        What Lodestone cannot do here

        These models are far smaller than the one on harmonism.io. They make mistakes and can state things the articles do not say — which is why nothing below the seven-billion class is offered here at all. When something matters, read the article: every reply lists the articles it drew on, and they open offline. In a crisis Lodestone gives you a helpline, not a conversation; it is a companion, not a clinician.
